HOPE Labs

One front for the HOPE Lab's tools on Grace. Sign in once, pick a tool, and it opens in a tab of its own: the page it always was, reached through one connection and one Duo approval.

What it does

The lab runs five tools on Grace, and each already serves a page of its own on a login node.

Opening one of them by hand takes a terminal, an SSH session, the command that starts it, a second session that forwards its port, and a Duo approval for each session. HOPE Labs signs in once, holds that one connection, and does the rest for whichever tool you pick: it starts the tool on the login node, waits for it to say it is ready, forwards a port to it, and shows it in a tab of its own.

The tools are not changed in any way. Each tab shows the tool's own page, with its own Docs, under a thin bar that reaches the other four. Nothing is installed on the cluster for HOPE Labs, and nothing of the tools is installed on your computer.

The page

  1. HOPE Labs, and under it the account you signed in with and the login node it is on.
  2. Tools and Results, the two views. Tools is where a session starts.
  3. The tools, each with a dot that is green while it is running. Click a name to open that tool's tab, starting the tool first if it is not up.
  4. The one thing to remember about cost: every tool runs under your own account, and the jobs it submits are charged to you.
  5. The categories. Pick one to show only those tools; pick it again, or All, to show every one.
  6. Search the tools matches what you type against each tool's name, what it does and its category.
  7. Docs, in the top right corner, opens these pages in a tab of their own.
  8. A block for each tool: what it does, whether it is running, and what it takes from another tool or leaves for the next one.
  9. Launch starts the tool and opens its tab. Once it is running the button says Open, and Stop appears beside it. The dashed buttons open the tool that usually comes next.
  10. The bar along the bottom: the version, the copyright, who does the work, and links to these pages and to How to cite.

A session, start to finish

  1. Start HOPE Labs on your computer and sign in to Grace with your NetID, your password and one Duo approval. The page opens in your browser by itself.
  2. Launch a tool. Its tab opens at once and fills in when the tool is ready. The first start of the day can take a minute while the tool's environment loads.
  3. Work in it exactly as you would without HOPE Labs: prepare, submit, follow, read the results.
  4. Hand the result on. Results lists the runs of every tool together, and the button beside a run opens the tool that takes it next, with the run's folder ready to copy.
  5. Sign out when you are done. The pages close and the connection ends; jobs already in the queue carry on.
